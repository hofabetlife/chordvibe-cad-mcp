## Summary
- Eliminated non-watertight boundary openings (`open_boundary_edges = 413..7471`, `genus = 68..737`), self-intersections (`1..129`), and needle/sliver triangles (`16.3%..35.1%` triangles with angle `< 5°`, `353..1492` with angle `< 1°`) produced by `cut_helical_groove` (`circular` and `metric_60` ISO 68-1 profiles) on internal nut threads (`T08_hex_nut_m10`, DIN 934 M10 hex nut) and spiral twist drills (`cylinder` + `cut_helical_groove` + `cone`).
- Implemented exact coaxial cylindrical/conical manifold meshing in `crates/mcp-cad-core/src/csg.rs` (`try_cut_coaxial_through_bore`, `try_union_coaxial_cone`, and `try_cut_coaxial_helical_groove`) backed by a 1-to-2 ring doubling transition (`add_doubling_ring_transition_2d`) in `crates/mcp-cad-core/src/primitives.rs` and an $O(N)$ 3D spatial grid for collinear canonical vertex snapping in `polygons_to_mesh`.
- All helical groove models (`T08_hex_nut_m10`, `HEX_NUT_MM`, `DRILL_BIT`, `DRILL_CONE` in both feature orders, and `qa_wide_scan` operations `cut_helical_groove_circular` / `cut_helical_groove_metric_60`) now pass `validate_mesh` with **`status = "PASS"`**, **`is_watertight = true`**, **`boundary_edges_count = 0`**, **`non_manifold_edges_count = 0`**, **`self_intersections_count = 0`**, **`lt_1_deg_count = 0`**, **`lt_5_deg_ratio = 0.00%`**, and exact topological genus (**`genus = 1.0`** for threaded hex nuts, **`genus = 0.0`** for fluted drills/cylinders).

## Root Cause
1. **Non-Watertight BSP-CSG on Swept Helical Cutters (`T08_hex_nut_m10`)**:
   - `create_helical_cutter_with_settings` generates a multi-turn swept tube (`~1400–2800` twisted triangles) whose inner cylindrical wall lies nearly tangent/coplanar to the bore wall (`r = 4.25 mm`) created by `cut_hole`.
   - Recursive 3D BSP polygon splitting (`BspNode::clip_polygons`) across hundreds of twisted helical facets accumulated floating-point grazing splits (`PLANE_EPS = 1e-5`) and unsealed T-junctions where helical crests crossed the bore wall and the top/bottom annular caps, leaving `413` open boundary edges (`genus = 68` in `bench_suite`) in SI units and `7471` open boundary edges (`129` self-intersections) in millimeter units.
2. **Sliver Triangles at Helical Groove Runout & Drill Tip (`drill_bit`)**:
   - Where the swept helical groove exited the flat end caps or intersected the conical drill point (`cone`), arbitrary plane-triangle clipping produced needle-shaped transition wedges with minimum angles `< 0.01°` (`16.3%–35.1%` triangles `< 5°`), failing `validate_mesh`.
